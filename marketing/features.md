<!-- feature-section -->
## Orders to Stamped.io

When an eligible Craft Commerce order reaches the configured state, queue its customer and line-item information for Stamped.io. Background delivery keeps Stamped API communication away from the customer’s payment response, while the store’s normal order workflow remains the source of truth. Configuration controls the account connection and eligible order behaviour.
